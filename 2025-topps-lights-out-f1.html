2025 Topps Lights Out Formula 1

The complete checklist — base set, parallel ladder with print runs, framed autographs and inserts.

57-card base 7 parallels · to Gold 1/1 26 autograph subjects Released Aug 29, 2025 Online exclusive

Topps' dark-mode online exclusive — a 57-card unnumbered base set across five themed categories, a seven-color parallel ladder topped by the metal-framed Gold 1/1, and autographs from 26 subjects spanning the F1 grid and the next F2 wave. Sold only through the Topps European store: 20 cards per box with two numbered parallels per box; autographs land in select boxes (odds undisclosed).

⭐ Key Cards

  • Kimi Antonelli Gold Frame 1/1 — the rookie chase of the set
  • Lewis Hamilton framed Drivers Autograph
  • Oliver Bearman — the only driver in all three F1 autograph categories (Drivers, Nero & Bright Future)
  • Max Verstappen Nero

✍️ Autographs — 26 subjects

  • Drivers Autographs (12) — Ocon, Colapinto, Russell, Hadjar, Antonelli, Stroll, Norris, Hamilton, Bearman, Piastri, Gasly, Tsunoda
  • Nero Autographs (5) — Russell, Norris, Hamilton, Bearman, Gasly

Same parallel ladder as the base cards, to the metal-framed Gold 1/1. Topps didn't publish autograph odds — they appear in select boxes.

🎴 Inserts & Subsets

  • Bright Future (6) — the rookie class: Colapinto, Bortoleto, Hadjar, Antonelli, Lawson, Bearman
  • Nero (10) — black-finish elite subset: Sainz, Alonso, Russell, Norris, Hamilton, Lawson, Verstappen, Hulkenberg, Bearman, Gasly
  • Next Gen F2 (6) — Dunne, Minì, Martí, Fornaroli, Verschoor, Martins
  • Records (5) — milestone cards for Verstappen, Alonso, Leclerc & Antonelli
  • Teams (10) — driver/team-principal trio cards, one per team
